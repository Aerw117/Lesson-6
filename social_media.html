Social media is a group of websites and apps that let people share things with each other over the Internet. After you create a free account, you can post words, pictures, and videos, and other people can see them, "like" them, comment on them, and pass them along to their own friends. Because a single post can be shared again and again, social media is one of the fastest and least expensive ways for a new company to let people know it exists.

Social Media Comparison

Instagram
Instagram is a photo and video sharing app owned by Meta. Businesses use it to show off products with eye-catching pictures, short "Reels" videos, and 24-hour "Stories." Hashtags and the Explore page help new people discover a brand, which makes Instagram a strong choice for building visual awareness, especially with younger audiences.

Source: Instagram_logo_2016.svg
LinkedIn
LinkedIn is a professional networking site owned by Microsoft. A company can create a free Company Page, post news and articles, advertise job openings, and connect with other businesses. It is the best fit for reaching professionals, partners, investors, and future employees, and for establishing a new company as credible and trustworthy.

Source: LinkedIn_logo_initials.png
Facebook
Facebook is the largest social network in the world, also owned by Meta. Businesses can create a free Page to share updates, photos, events, and reviews, and can run low-cost ads targeted by location, age, and interests. Its huge and varied audience makes it a good all-around tool for introducing a new company to its local community.
